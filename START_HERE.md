## Setup requirements

- Access to a Claude Pro account – Claude Code means that anyone can set this up or modify it as needs be.
- A domain name – ideally something neutral and descriptive, like aisafetyvoteralliance.ie / .com / .de / .eu etc.
- A business email account – I used Google Workspace for this (needed for the sending/contact address specifically), but anything that enables automated email sending should do.
- A GitHub account – the free tier is perfectly fine.
- A Supabase account – the free tier is again perfectly fine.

Total monthly cost should be less than £30, £20 of which is the Claude Pro account which could be cancelled after the site is set up.

## Style considerations

- I'd recommend adapting the colour palette of your ASVA to match your national colours. That tends to feel more 'right' with people and will allow sister ASVAs to feel distinctive but coordinated.
- I recommend sticking with something like the hex map where you can, i.e. something which shows voting districts equally. This means that city and rural areas stay the same size. It's likely variations of these maps were produced at your last national election – you can show the map to Claude and Claude can produce one just like it for your ASVA.
- If at all possible, make sure the site remains sleek and feels well designed. This will significantly improve the odds that a visitor trusts the site enough to pledge their vote.
- Make sure the site is optimised for mobile visitors, very many visitors will be using a mobile or tablet to visit you.

## Marketing

This is very much a work in progress, but hopefully we can support each other and find out what works and what doesn't. At the moment my ideas are:

- Reach out to AI safety creators or other orgs in this space. Even a footnote in a newsletter could bring many more voters online.
- Create stickers and/or badges that are given if people pledge. This is mostly for you or enthusiasts to have on you and remove a slight barrier to getting people to pledge.
- Create pretty and shareable social media images/posts that people will want to share.

## Coordination between ASVAs

I suggest that any founders for a country are part of a WhatsApp so we can quickly and easily share what works, what doesn't and what we need help on. We can flex into more coordination as and when we need to. If you aren't sure how to tackle a problem then just reach out – more people = more solutions.

## Legal and compliance

You will be handling personal data when you accept people's names, emails and locations. The current data privacy notice is GDPR compliant but might need tweaking or adaptation based on the rules of your country. If at all possible, get a legal professional to review your compliance notice. Any trouble finding one, contact me and/or the Pause community, it should be a short job for any lawyer.
