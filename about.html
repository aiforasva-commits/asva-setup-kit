About Us

The AI Safety Voter Alliance ("ASVA") exists to answer a simple question: how do ordinary people make sure that AI is properly regulated?

What is ASVA

ASVA is a civic organisation designed to give AI safety voters real political leverage. We do this by:

  • Building a voter bloc of people who pledge to prioritise AI safety when they vote
  • Grading MPs and candidates using an AI Safety Scorecard
  • Rewarding or withholding electoral support based on that Scorecard

For detailed information on ASVA, please see our Founding Statement.

Why voting matters

Under the UK's electoral system, constituencies are often won or lost by narrow margins. This means a small but committed group of voters in any given constituency can wield outsized influence - provided politicians know their votes hinge on one issue.

ASVA is building just such a group, with the aim of making AI safety a priority across the political spectrum.
